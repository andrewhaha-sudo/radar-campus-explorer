## What this repository actually does

- Outdoor and indoor illustration map, collision, stairs, and A* walking: `src/components/map`.
- Room photos stored on the device, plus an optional published manifest: `src/lib/room-photos.ts`.
- Device trainer card and optional separate student account: `src/components/student`, `supabase/migrations/202611020001_student_profiles.sql`.
- Outdoor GPS follow with device calibration, accuracy limits, and no peer broadcast: `src/lib/location-sync.ts`.
- Building floor and room list taken only from the illustration data: `illustrationBuilding`.

## DEMO or not configured

- One-point demo calibration and its 8-pixels-per-meter north-up scale.
- Device role label. It is not the six school roles and it does not authorize anything.
- Student cloud accounts. They require the separate Supabase project and are not a school workspace.
- PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, weather effects, alerts, SOS, guardians, inspections, vision models, and emergency numbers are not configured. No endpoint was invented for them.
