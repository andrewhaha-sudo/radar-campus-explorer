This bridge is original RADAR integration code. It imports the unmodified public APIs of:

- LifeRouter v1.0.0, https://github.com/Kentalives/LifeRouter,
  commit 99e468e4ae7b382832dcf0026914c4d08ce53ff0, Apache-2.0.
- geotruth v1.0.0, https://github.com/midtxwn/geotruth, Apache-2.0.
- NATS server/client, https://github.com/nats-io, Apache-2.0.

The Apache-2.0 license is included in licenses/LifeRouter-APACHE-2.0.txt.
Additional dependencies and exact versions are recorded in go.mod and go.sum.
Their source distributions retain their respective copyright and license notices.
No Pathfinder or other AGPL/OSL source was copied into this integration.
